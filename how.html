What we count

Ten numbers describe the whole Earth at every moment. They measure different things, so each keeps its own units and none is added to another. Two more describe a single system, such as one cell, one brain or one supercomputer, instead of the whole planet.

Part 5

How the numbers are built

The numbers are not separate guesses. Each one is tied to a measured input and an equation, and the totals must add up: living carbon must sum to 550 billion tonnes, life’s power must stay below what sunlight provides, and every operation must pay at least the smallest energy cost physics allows.

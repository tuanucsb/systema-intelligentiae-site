04 · Biophysics of anti-entropic order

Intelligence is the local opposite of the Second Law of Thermodynamics

Left alone in a closed box, the Second Law guarantees that thermal jostling scrambles every code, flattens every voltage and erodes every monument toward maximum entropy. Intelligence never violates the global Second Law—instead, it acts as an open-system Maxwell’s Demon: harvesting free energy, compressing sensory regularities into predictive models, and executing control actions that pump entropy out as heat so local matter becomes progressively more organized.

Without free-energy input and error-correcting feedback, membrane voltages collapse in milliseconds, dynamic RAM loses its charge in 64 milliseconds, flash SSDs leak in years, and unprotected DNA in bone fragments with a 521-year half-life.

Intelligent Negentropy Pump · Open system

Predictive control concentrates local order

dSlocal / dt = Ṡdecay − kB ln 2 · İcontrol(P, K) < 0   (with Q̇out/T ≥ |ΔṠlocal|)

By paying at least Rolf Landauer’s thermodynamic floor (kBT ln 2 ≈ 2.87×10⁻²¹ J/bit at 15 °C) and using stored models (K) to steer action, cells, brains and civilizations hold macromolecules, connectomes and global archives far from equilibrium across millions of years.

05 · Unified synthesis metric across 68 moments

A multi-metric index of planetary intelligence—and its exponential rise on Earth

On our six single-formula test page, raw event totals (Doing) look flat since the dinosaurs because 3.1×10³⁰ bacteria execute >99.9999% of cellular chemical reactions. Yet Earth clearly became vastly more organized. Why? Because anti-entropic intelligence expands multiplicatively across five orthogonal dimensions we have tracked: free-energy budget, non-redundant knowledge, multi-substrate computing layers, single-agent cognitive concentration, and networked working memory.

Planetary Organizational Intelligence Index · anchored to 4.10 Ga = 1 (0 orders of magnitude)

Methodological note & scope caveat: Every factor is normalized by its Hadean origin value at 4.10 billion years ago, making ℐorg(t) strictly dimensionless (ℐorg(t₀) = 1) and free of unit-mixing errors. Crucially, Φsub(t) = Πs(1 + Cs(t)) multiplies across the five physical computing layers (biochemical, bioelectric, neural, classical silicon and learned AI), so unlocking a faster substrate amplifies planetary capability without erasing older layers. Like the Home stack, this index describes the planet’s cumulative anti-entropic organization over time—never the worth or rank of any species, person or machine.

Why growth is super-exponential in calendar years: the doubling clock itself sped up 64-million-fold

Each time Earth invented a faster way to store and route information—DNA, myelinated brains, speech and writing, industrial print and telegraphy, then silicon and AI—that substrate accelerated the search for the next one, compressing the time needed to double ℐorg(t) from tens of millions of years to roughly twelve months.

Atlas of minds

The stack is made of things

Each dot is one system from the catalog behind this site: a cell, an animal, an archive, a society, a chip, a neural net. It sits where its kind first appeared and at the number of operations it does each second, in the colour of the layer of the thinking stack it lives in.

Map of systems

Both axes go up in steps of ×10. A dot one grid line higher does ten times more each second; a dot one grid line to the left first appeared ten times longer ago.

What we have measured, and for whom

Each cell counts the systems of that kind with at least one sourced measurement in that dimension. Darker means a larger share of that kind is covered. The pale cells are the gaps.

About

A natural history of thinking matter

Systema Intelligentiae tells how Earth learned to think: how memory, wiring and computing were built up over 4.1 billion years, from chemistry in a rock pore to today’s data centers. Every page draws on one open census, so the story, the tree and the data always agree.

How it was made

  1. 64 moments. Earth’s history is split into 64 moments, from 4.1 billion years ago to 2026. Forty of them are stops on the journey, each with one star creature.
  2. Ten numbers. Each moment is described by ten quantities measured separately: DNA, working and stored memory, biological, neural and digital throughput, power and persistence. They are never added into one score.
  3. Sources first. Each number traces to published estimates in the sources register, which you can open in the Data Lab.
  4. Honest uncertainty. Today’s values carry 95% ranges from a 10,000-draw Monte Carlo. Older moments are labelled by how direct their evidence is (how sure are we?).
  5. Checked twice. A validator checks the census, the stories and the image credits against each other every time the site is built.

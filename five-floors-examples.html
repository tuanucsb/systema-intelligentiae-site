Earth learned to think five times

Each new way arrived faster than the last, and none of the old ones went away. Below are three figures built to tell that one story.

Example A

The building

Five floors, read from the ground up, with a one-day clock beside them. Best at "nothing was replaced": the old floors are still holding the new ones up. Click a floor.

Five floors of thinking

If all of Earth’s history were one day, the ground floor opened at midnight. The top three floors were all built in the last 70 seconds.

Ground · 4.1 billion years ago
Example B

The staircase

Five steps climbing left to right, with the wait before each step written on it. Best at "faster each time": the waits are the first thing you read.

Each step came sooner than the last

The first step took 3.5 billion years to follow. The latest took 67.

Example C

One day, zoomed in three times

Three time bars, each a close-up of the end of the one above. Best at showing how late everything human is, with no special scale to learn. It is the most chart-like of the three.

You have to zoom in twice to find the machines

Every bar is drawn to scale. Each one magnifies the last sliver of the bar above it.

The research behind the site

Six manuscripts underpin Systema Intelligentiae: one key paper that builds the census of Earth, and five companions that each take one question further. Every paper here comes with an independent check: its main numbers recomputed from the project’s data, its reasoning and its conclusions judged, and its contradictions listed.
